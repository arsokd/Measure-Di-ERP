Calculation Formulas & Financial Engine

Total Project Revenue: Σ (Milestone Invoices + T&M Billings + Approved Change Orders)
Total Direct Expenses: Σ (Direct Labor + Subcontractors + Direct Materials)
Total Allocated Shared Expenses: Σ (Pro-rata Travel & Lodging + Local Conveyance + Overhead)
Gross Profit & Margin %: Gross Profit = Total Revenue - (Direct Exp + Allocated Shared Exp)
Margin % = (Gross Profit / Total Revenue) * 100
